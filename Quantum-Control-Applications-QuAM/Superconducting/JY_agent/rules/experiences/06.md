## Lesson — sparse logarithmic sampling can alias Ramsey oscillations

- Snapshots `903`–`905` used 100-point logarithmic sweeps and produced
  nonphysical, uncertain, under-covered, or non-reproducible fits for q3/q4/q5.
- A 2 MHz detuning has a 500 ns period. A 600-point linear 16–60000 ns sweep
  (about 100 ns spacing) resolved that oscillation and converted all three fits
  into stable accepted results. Prefer enough linear-time samples to resolve
  the detuning period whenever sparse/log sampling leaves unresolved bands or
  contradictory repeated fits.
