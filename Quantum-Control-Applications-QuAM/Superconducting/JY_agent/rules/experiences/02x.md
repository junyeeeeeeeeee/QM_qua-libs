## Lesson — 2026-09-02 20:20 Asia/Taipei

- Device/project: `as-qpu-10qV2_agent`
- Observation: protected-node fitting treats readout `upconverter_frequency`
  (IF = 0, detuning `−IF`) as a resonance whenever a wide 02x/02a window
  includes the LO. Operator-supplied resonator RF in `state.json` is already
  close; 02x only fine-tunes a few MHz.
- Recovery: keep `frequency_span_in_mhz` at most 60 MHz (default 20). Never
  widen until the LO is inside the sweep. After a passing 02x, the next node
  is `02c`, not `02a`.
